Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

LeadHunter AI respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we handle your login profile, search logs, and saved business leads.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and store only the minimal necessary information to execute local lead generation tasks:

  • Account Registration Details: Username, email, and hashed password.
  • Business Leads and Search History: Saved listings fetched via SerpApi mapping.
  • Outreach Logs: A record of custom messages generated and emails dispatched.

2. Credential Security & Local Storage

To maximize security, third-party credentials (such as your private SerpApi key, Gemini API key, and SMTP login details) are processed in-memory and can be stored in your browser's local storage rather than on our backend database, ensuring complete client-side control.

3. Data Deletion & Portability

In accordance with GDPR compliance standards, you possess the right to portability and the right to be forgotten:

  • Export Data: You can download all your stored records (user profile, saved leads, history) at any time from your settings console.
  • Delete Account: Triggering the account deletion will instantly and permanently delete all your information from our database server.

4. Third-Party Services

Our service communicates with SerpApi and Google Gemini platforms to execute searches and draft content. Please review their respective privacy agreements regarding external query analytics.
