Data Protection Policy & GDPR Disclosure

Privacy Policy

DOCUMENT REF: LH-PRIV-2026-V2 | EFFECTIVE DATE: JUNE 12, 2026
DATA PRIVACY COMMITMENT: LEADHUNTER AI IS COMMITTED TO EXCELLENCE IN USER PRIVACY AND GDPR DATA PROTECTION. THIS DOCUMENT STIPULATES OUR METHODS OF PROCESSING, SECURING, SCRAPING, AND DISCLOSING OPERATIONAL ASSETS AND PERSONAL IDENTIFYING INFORMATION (PII).

1. Information Processing & Data Minimization

The Platform strictly collects and stores only the minimum necessary operational data required to coordinate lead tracking pipelines:

  • Account Parameters: Username, contact telephone numbers, email details, and cryptographically hashed passwords.
  • Scouted Leads & Directories: Public local listings data (e.g. name, reviews, business address, coordinates, active URLs) retrieved via Google Maps APIs.
  • Outreach Logs: Metrics of generated outreach drafts, dispatched email dates, open tracking status counts, and click analytics.

2. Local Credential Encryption & Sandbox Policy

To establish enterprise-grade security, all external API keys (Google Maps API keys, Gemini AI private keys) and SMTP sender accounts credentials are processed in-memory or saved directly inside the Operator's local browser storage sandbox. These private keys are never permanently persisted on our backend database, ensuring complete client-side control and mitigating the risk of central server database leaks.

3. GDPR Compliance: Portability and Right of Erasure

In full compliance with General Data Protection Regulation standards (specifically Articles 17 and 20), the Platform guarantees the following user rights:

  • Data Portability: Operators can trigger a complete export request. The Platform dynamically packages and downloads all saved records, users data, leads pipelines, and tracking metrics in a structured JSON schema via the `/api/config/export-data` endpoint.
  • Right of Erasure (Cascading Cleanup): Triggering the account deletion parameter in settings instantly deletes the Operator profile. The server cascades the delete command, permanently purging all associated history, leads, tracking pixels logs, and configuration records from the database.

4. Third-Party Sub-processors & External Flows

Our search mechanisms communicate with Google Maps database directories to extract local businesses info, and transmit sanitized prompts to Google Gemini AI to compose cold outreach letters. Operators are advised to review the specific privacy policies of Google and Google Cloud Services to examine how external API data queries are processed.

5. SMTP Email Tracking & Mockup Redirections Disclosure

1. The Platform integrates outreach telemetry to monitor lead engagement. Dispatched cold emails contain a transparent tracking pixel (1x1 GIF) to monitor read status and open count increments.

2. Outreach messages containing custom portfolio link references are wrapped inside tracking redirect routes. When a recipient clicks the link, the tracking server registers the click event, automatically elevates the corresponding lead's status to **INTERESTED**, and redirects the client browser to the destination URL. Operators must ensure that their cold email lists conform to local permissions regulations before executing campaigns.

6. CSRF Shields & Browser Cookies

The Platform uses standard session cookies and enforces secure CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery) verification tokens for all status-modifying operations (POST, PUT, DELETE). Cookies are flagged with secure, SameSite=Lax headers to prevent session hijacking and cross-site scripting vulnerabilities.
